CV Bio

I am a Ph.D. candidate (direct track) in Electrical Engineering at Tel Aviv University, advised by Dan Raviv in the Geometric Deep Learning Lab. My research studies the emergence of structural low rank and spectral bias in deep learning, and how they shape generalization, forgetting, and optimization. I use these insights to develop principled methods for applications spanning classification, language and vision models, implicit neural representations (INRs) and graphics, forgetting and model merging, and post-training.

Alongside my Ph.D., I am an AI Research Scientist Intern in the Multimodal AI group at IBM Research, led by Eli Schwartz, where I work on post-training for vision-language models (VLMs). Previously, I received a B.Sc. in Applied Mathematics and completed my M.Sc. thesis work in Electrical Engineering, both at Tel Aviv University, and led the deep learning group at Dtect Vision.

Always happy to discuss low-rank structure and spectral bias in deep learning; feel free to reach out via email.
